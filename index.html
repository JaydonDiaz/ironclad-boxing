Our Mission

Boxing as an Art Form

At Ironclad Boxing Club, we believe boxing is more than a sport — it's a discipline that builds character, mental toughness, and lifelong resilience.

We train boxers of all ages and skill levels, from beginners discovering the sport for the first time to competitive athletes pushing their limits. Our programs emphasize fitness, self-defense, and overall well-being in a supportive, family-oriented environment.

Juniors (6) through Masters (35+)

Non-Profit Initiative

Ironclad Boxing Youth Champs

IYC is our community-driven non-profit dedicated to supporting under-resourced youth through the discipline and values of boxing.

We believe every child deserves access to quality coaching and mentorship — regardless of their economic situation. Through IYC, we provide subsidized programs, equipment, and coaching to youth in our community who need it most.
