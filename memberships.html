Good to Know

Common Questions

Do I need experience to join?

No experience needed for most classes. New members are encouraged to start on Monday, Tuesday, or Wednesday. Complete beginners may want to book the $45 1-on-1 intro session first.

What's included in all plans?

All membership plans give you unlimited access to every group class — Adult Technique, Junior Boxing, Youth Boxing, Youth Sparring, and Adult Sparring (Thursday). No per-class fees.

Are family discounts available?

Yes — group discounts are available for families enrolling 2 or more members. Contact us directly at (407) 555-0142 when signing up to get the family rate.

How does the 6-month plan work?

The 6-month plan charges $145 automatically each month for six months via the card on file. Registration is required before checkout. It's the most cost-effective option if you're committed to training.

Ready to Start Training?

Reach out and we'll get you set up with the right plan for your goals.
