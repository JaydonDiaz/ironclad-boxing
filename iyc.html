Our Story

More Than Boxing

IYC — Ironclad Youth Champs, Inc. — is a 501(c)(3) non-profit born from the belief that every child, regardless of their economic situation, deserves access to quality coaching and mentorship.

Founded alongside Ironclad Boxing Club in Oviedo, FL, IYC bridges the gap for youth who otherwise couldn't afford structured athletic and personal development programs. We use boxing not as an end goal, but as a vehicle for life-changing discipline, education, and community.

Serving youth ages 8–17 in Central Florida
Focused on low-income and at-risk populations
Registered 501(c)(3) — donations are tax-deductible
Based at 4810 Vanguard St #210, Oviedo, FL 32765

What We Provide

Beyond the Ring

IYC delivers a complete program — from technical boxing skills to life skills that equip youth for every challenge they'll face.

Boxing Technique

Proper stance, footwork, combinations, and fundamentals taught by expert coaches with real competitive experience.

Discipline & Respect

Boxing demands structure and self-control. IYC instills values of respect, accountability, and personal responsibility that last a lifetime.

Education

Sessions include educational topics beyond boxing — bullying prevention, depression awareness, stress management, and resilience training.

Mentorship

Youth are paired with caring adult coaches who provide consistent guidance, support, and positive role models in their lives.

Eligibility

Who Can Apply?

IYC spots are filled on a rolling basis depending on available funding. If you believe your child qualifies, reach out — we want to hear from you.

Requirements

Ages 8–17 years old
Resident of Central Florida
Under-resourced household — low-income or facing economic hardship
At-risk youth facing social, emotional, or community challenges
Availability subject to program funding

What's Included

Subsidized or sponsored class access
Expert coaching from the Ironclad Boxing staff
Full boxing technique curriculum
Educational workshops and life-skills sessions
Ongoing mentorship and community

How to Apply

1
Send an Email

Email us at info@ironcladboxing.com to request an application. Tell us a little about your child and your situation.

2
Complete Application

We'll send over an application form. Fill it out and return it so we can review eligibility and check program availability.

3
Get Started

Once approved, your child joins the program. We'll handle the rest — class schedule, coaching, and everything in between.
